Field Support Playbook

On-Site Troubleshooting Protocol

When dispatched to plant facilities, Batrisyia implements a systematic four-stage methodology balancing safety, sensor telemetry, and hydraulic verification.

01

Lock-Out Tag-Out & Risk Baseline

Verification of positive isolation, depressurization checks, toxic vapor venting confirmation before physical line entry.

02

P&ID Walk-down & Instrumentation Interrogation

Tracing upstream/downstream manifolds, checking impulse lines for plugging, validating transmitter zero-drift against field gauges.

03

Thermodynamic Simulation Cross-Check

Running rapid Aspen HYSYS back-calculations with observed pressures and temperatures to detect phase change or fouling.

04

Standardized Handover & Root-Cause Report

Drafting concise bilingual (English/Malay or Japanese summary) corrective reports detailing immediate remedial actions and long-term preventative maintenance.
